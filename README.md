# Career Docs Generator

Career Docs Generator is a web application that helps people create polished, professional career documents — CVs and letters — with the help of AI. Instead of starting from a blank page or wrestling with formatting, users fill in guided forms, let AI refine their wording, and download a ready-to-send document in minutes.

## What It Does

- **Build CVs:** Enter your details through structured forms and choose from several professional templates — Classic, Modern, Minimal, and Professional. AI can suggest stronger summaries, sharpen experience descriptions, and recommend relevant skills.
- **Write Letters:** Create cover letters, job and internship applications, recommendation letters, and resignation letters. Provide a little context and the AI drafts the letter body for you.
- **Improve Your Writing:** Enhance any section with AI, or ask for alternative phrasings until the wording feels right.
- **Export Anywhere:** Download finished documents as PDF or Word files, ready to email, upload, or print.
- **Keep Everything in One Place:** Sign in with email or Google to save, revisit, and update all your documents from a personal dashboard.

## Who It's For

Students, graduates, and professionals who want to present themselves well — whether applying for a first internship, changing jobs, or recommending a colleague — without spending hours on writing and formatting.

## Built With

React on the frontend, Django on the backend, and DeepSeek for AI-powered writing assistance.
